!Before you publish

  • One mapping applies to every user. Conditional mappings are not supported.
  • You cannot change the mapping after the connection is published.
  • Regular expressions extract, they never transform. They cannot change case, reorder characters, or add anything that is not already in the value — so if what you expect is not literally inside the property, no expression can produce it.
  • Regular expressions are not supported on the ME-ID path. Microsoft Entra user properties go into the formula whole.
  • Replace the sample values with data your connector really returns. Service accounts, contractors and merged domains are where a mapping usually breaks.
  • The wizard Preview shows five random users. Green there does not prove every user maps.
